Every employee ranked on margin (revenue impact vs cost), build effort (Evolv slots to stand up), and compliance risk (regulatory / account-ban exposure). This drives the sell order: lead with money found, gate the high-compliance Hunters.

The Play
Recommended sell motion

Rule of the stack: lead with money found, not money spent. REVA pays for the Core, the Core proves ROI, ROI earns the right to sell Hunters.

Phase 1 · Land

REVA → Conversion Core

Open on REVA — "let's get paid from leads you already have." Attach FLASH + BOOKER as the Core. Every lead now answered in <60s and booked. Non-negotiable foundation.

Phase 2 · Prove

OWEN + first Nurture

Bundle OWEN early — makes ROI visible weekly, stops churn. Add NADIA / REMI / JADA: cheap, low-risk, captures the 95% the Core can't close today.

Phase 3 · Expand

Hunters, lowest-risk first

Start with LIAM (proven Amy model). Then DANTE / MILA as the market fits. SCOTT last and only behind compliance sign-off. ARIA for durable-brand clients.

Phase 4 · Operate

Ops as deals scale

VERA once deal volume justifies it (replaces $300–500/deal TC cost). IRIS anytime as an easy time-recovery add-on.

01

Never open on a Hunter

Cold lead-gen with no Core = leads leaking out the bottom of the bucket. Core first, always.

02

Gate the high-compliance Hunters

SCOTT / MILA require explicit client sign-off on DNC/TCPA and Meta Housing policy before a single message or ad goes out. Protect the client and Evolv.
