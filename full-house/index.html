The only reason anyone pays us

There is exactly one reasona cinema hires an agency:it wants more money than it has.

Not impressions. Not followers. Not a nicer website. Money arrives through three doors and there is no fourth: more people through the door, more spend per person, or less cost to get either. Every service below is attached to one of those three doors, and every service below is priced against what it returns.

1

More admissions

Social regia, paid media, online ticketing, the database, the AI agent. Everything that converts a person who was going to stay home.

2

More per admission

The bar. It is the highest-margin thing a cinema sells and almost nobody markets it. A €0.40 move on spend-per-head beats most ticket campaigns.

3

Less cost to get both

The data layer and the automations. Wasted ad spend recovered, staff hours returned, decisions made on numbers instead of instinct.
