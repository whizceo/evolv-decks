The 30-Day Build

Action plan. Signed and paid 24 August 2026. Day 1 is Tuesday 25 August. Day 30 is Wednesday 23 September.

Phase 1 builds the system. It does not run a campaign — volume starts after the Day-30 confirmation. Everything below exists to make the seven completion points true, on the record, by 23 September.

The one thing that cannot slip

Mailbox warm-up takes three full weeks and cannot be compressed. Domains must be bought and authenticated on Day 3 at the latest, or Day 30 arrives with cold mailboxes and point 4 is not true. Every other task has slack. This one has none.

Week 1 — Foundation

Lock the profile, buy the infrastructure

Day 1–7 · Tue 25 Aug – Mon 31 Aug
  • Day 1. Onboarding call. Issac books it himself. Output: target counties, seller-side profile in his words, the exclusion list (his contacts, CRM, prior prospects, referral sources).
  • Day 2–3. Profile written up and sent back for written confirmation. Nothing is treated as approved because he did not reply — it gets confirmed or it waits.
  • Day 3. Buy dedicated domains. Create mailboxes. SPF, DKIM, DMARC set and verified. Warm-up starts the same day.
  • Day 4–7. Source selection: which public and licensed owner records cover the target counties, at what refresh, at what cost. Sourcing pipeline scaffolded.

Point 1 closes Day 5. Warm-up clock starts Day 3 and runs to Day 24.

Week 2 — Data

Sourcing and enrichment

Day 8–14 · Tue 1 Sep – Mon 7 Sep
  • Day 8–11. Sourcing pipeline runs live against the counties. Deduplication and scoring built against the confirmed profile. Exclusion list wired in at the source, not at send time.
  • Day 12. First scored owner list delivered to Issac. This is the first thing he can hold. Point 2 closes here.
  • Day 13–14. Enrichment layer: owner identity, contact details, business attributes, property ownership. Verification step in front of every contact, suppression automatic.

Parallel, low intensity: LinkedIn seat connected on Issac's identity. Mailboxes continue warming.

Week 3 — Voice

Sequences and platform

Day 15–21 · Tue 8 Sep – Mon 14 Sep
  • Day 15–17. Enrichment and verification finished end to end on the real list. Point 3 closes Day 17.
  • Day 16–19. Email and LinkedIn sequences drafted in Issac's voice — his language, his positioning, seller-side.
  • Day 19. Sequences sent to Issac for approval. Revision round built into the week.
  • Day 20–21. Approved sequences loaded into the sending platform. Volume pacing configured. Point 5 closes Day 21.

Week 4 — Handover

CRM, AIOS, confirmation

Day 22–30 · Tue 15 Sep – Wed 23 Sep
  • Day 22–26. CRM rebuilt seller-side: pipeline, stages, routing, follow-up. First Contact and attribution recording configured. Dashboard built and connected. Point 6 closes Day 26.
  • Day 24. Warm-up complete on every mailbox. Sending infrastructure verified end to end. Point 4 closes.
  • Day 27–29. AIOS instance handed over. Content foundation delivered: positioning, pillars, formats, plus the frameworks and publishing tools. Point 7 closes Day 29.
  • Day 30 — Wed 23 Sep. Walkthrough call. Issac confirms the seven points in writing.
