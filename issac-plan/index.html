Broker Social Solutions × Evolv AI — Phase 1

The 30-Day Build

Every task, who owns it, and what makes it finished. Signed and paid 24 August 2026. Day 1 is Tuesday 25 August. Day 30 is Wednesday 23 September.

Phase 1 builds the system. It does not run a campaign — volume starts after the Day-30 confirmation, on infrastructure that has been warm for a week by then. Everything below exists to make the seven completion points true, on the record, by 23 September.

WHIZ — owner, client-facing DATA — sourcing, enrichment, scoring INFRA — domains, mailboxes, deliverability COPY — sequences, content CRM — pipeline, attribution, dashboard ISSAC — client input

The seven completion points

What has to be true on Day 30Closes
1The profile is locked. Ideal customer profile defined, written down and confirmed by Issac. His own existing contacts, CRM records, prior prospects and referral sources permanently excluded from all outreach.Day 5
2The sourcing layer is built. Pipeline runs against the target counties, pulling owner records from the agreed public and licensed sources, deduplicating and scoring them against the confirmed profile. First scored owner list delivered.Day 12
3The enrichment and verification layer is built. Owner identity, contact details, business attributes and property ownership appended, every contact verified before it can be used, anyone who should not be contacted removed automatically.Day 17
4The sending infrastructure is built. Dedicated domains and mailboxes acquired and authenticated with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, warm-up running on every mailbox, sending platform configured with volume pacing, LinkedIn seat connected on Issac's identity.Day 3 → 24
5The sequences are written and loaded. Email and LinkedIn sequences written in Issac's voice, approved by Issac, loaded ready to run.Day 21
6The CRM and reporting layer is built. CRM rebuilt seller-side — pipeline, stages, routing and follow-up — with First Contact and attribution recording configured, and the dashboard built and connected.Day 26
7The AIOS instance and content foundation are delivered. Client-specific AIOS instance handed over. Content foundation delivered: positioning, pillars and formats agreed, with the frameworks and publishing tools that go with them.Day 29
The one thing that cannot slip

Mailbox warm-up takes three full weeks and cannot be compressed, bought or faked. Domains must be purchased and authenticated on Day 3 at the latest, or Day 30 arrives with cold mailboxes and point 4 is not true. Every other task in this plan has slack. This one has none — which is why Day 2 carries a hard approval deadline on Issac's side and nothing else does.

How this is run

  • One channel. Everything about this build happens in the shared channel. A decision made anywhere else gets written back into it the same day.
  • Friday update, every Friday. What closed, what is next, what is waiting on him, and whether the Day 30 date still holds. Sent whether or not there is good news.
  • Points close in public. Each of the seven is marked complete in the channel with its evidence attached, not silently on a project board.
  • The plan is the record. If a date moves, this page changes and the change is announced. No quiet reslotting.
Issac decides whether the seven points are true. No other standard may be introduced by either Party. On his written confirmation, Phase 2 starts automatically — the retainer plus the agreed share of net commission, with no second signature. Either side can leave on thirty days' notice.
