04The club

A landmark of
Milanese nightlife.

Since 2005, The Club has been one of the addresses that define how Milan goes out. Corso Garibaldi 97 — Brera — behind a door that has hosted worldwide famous DJs, the official parties of Design Week and Fashion Week, and an international jet set that keeps coming back.

Two bar zones, a complete sound and video system, 200 seated and over 350 standing. Every night of the week has its own name, its own crowd, its own music.
