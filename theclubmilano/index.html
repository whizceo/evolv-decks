01 — The idea

Brera, Milano

Five nights.
One address.
Every one of them
has a name.

Since 2005, The Club has been one of the addresses that define how Milan goes out. Corso Garibaldi 97 — behind a door that has hosted worldwide famous DJs, the official parties of Design Week and Fashion Week, and an international jet set that keeps coming back.

Two bar zones. A complete sound and video system. Two hundred seated, over three hundred and fifty standing. Every night of the week has its own crowd and its own music.
