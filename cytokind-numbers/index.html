How it is counted and paid

  1. A unit counts when it is invoiced. Not when the prescription is signed, not when it ships.
  2. Counting window: 1 August 2026 – 1 January 2027, plus a 30-day tail to 31 January. If the count sits at 96 on 1 January and reaches 100 on 15 January, that milestone pays.
  3. Every unit counts. No source attribution, no split between channels — the number is the number.
  4. Milestones are payable on the earlier of: cash received for the units that triggered them, or 60 days from the trigger. Nothing is due before the money is in, up to that limit.
  5. Milestone amounts settle across the 60 days following the trigger, not in a single payment.
  6. The five-month term is fixed. That is what carries the build at no fee.
