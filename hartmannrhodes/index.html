⚡ The hard part — and the moat

We find the owner, not just the business.

"60+ and ready to exit" isn't a field in any database — which is exactly why generic lead lists fail you. So we don't buy the signal, we assemble it: how long the business has operated (25+ years usually means an owner near retirement), ownership records from state filings, and a public-records age check tied to the owner's name.

Most vendors stop at the company. We don't stop until we know who's behind it — and how close they are to wanting out.

That's the part LinkedIn scrapers and cold-email shops won't do.
