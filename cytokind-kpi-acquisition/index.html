03

The levers

22 inputs · M measured, sourced · A assumed, must be replaced

Pull anything. The model recomputes on every pixel of movement. Hover a source badge to see where the number came from. A value that has been moved off its Today baseline turns blue.

04

Marginal ROI — what to attack next

Every lever improved by one increment in isolation, ranked by gross contribution

This is the panel that decides the month. The model improves each lever on its own — by 10% of its current value, or one step of that lever, whichever is the larger move — holds everything else still, and reports the change in monthly revenue net of any extra spend it costs. The top row is the constraint. Re-read it every time the numbers move, because it moves too.

05

The creative ledger

One row per creative · every cell editable · verdict computed

The ads seat cannot return a lesson if creatives are not named and measured individually. Every creative is logged as angle_persona, and the ledger issues a verdict against the targets you set — so killing an ad is a rule, not an argument.

06

The content ledger

Organic · unpaid · the same discipline, no media cost

Organic content is measured on one number: contacts per 1,000 views. It converts attention that costs nothing into the same funnel the ads feed, so a post that produces contacts is worth more than a post that produces views. Fifteen reels have run. None produced a contact.

07

Cash already inside the building

One-time · not part of the monthly model above

Before a single new contact is bought, there is money sitting in the pipe that has already been paid for. This is the cheapest revenue on the page and it needs no ad spend, no new creative and no build.
