What does not exist at all

  • Any post-submit routing
  • A new-lead alert (SMS or push)

  • Form-abandonment tracking
  • Call recording

Panel D

The arithmetic of the gap

All figures on the $2,000 floor unit — the cheapest device CytoKind sells — so every number here is a floor, not an estimate.

And the marginal read, from the acquisition model built on these same inputs: a single point of improvement on “contacts routed onward after submit” — a lever sitting at zero — is worth more than any other lever on the board. Every traffic lever in the model returns nothing, because the human leg is already saturated at one person and one phone. The constraint is arithmetic, not rhetoric: it is not traffic, and it is not effort.

The one line

The ads work. The page works. Then the funnel stops at the submit button — and everything between there and the sale is one person on a phone, at one in twenty, against twenty arrivals a day.

Nothing happens between “submit” and “a human calls.” That gap is thirty-four touchpoints wide, it has no owner, and the fix for its largest single leak has been built and sitting in a folder for weeks.
