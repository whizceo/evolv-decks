The reconciliation

Why the dashboard and the sales record disagree

This is the single finding that explains the rest. It is a chain of four dependent objects, and the first one in the chain has never executed.

1
The pipeline automation has never runCustomer Pipeline — Lead to Order Confirmed is in state Inactive. Contacts entered: 0. It is the only automation in the account labelled with both business goals.
2
So the goal object was never fedMontly Target / August Sale reads 2 of 20 conversions and total value $0 USD. Its target end date is blank — the list view renders it as 01/01/1970, the Unix epoch, meaning it was never set.
3
And no sale has ever been attributedThe Revenue column returns “--” on all 11 campaigns ever sent, across all time. There is no revenue attribution configured anywhere in the account.
4
Which is why the two numbers differThe dashboard counts what passed through an automation that is switched off. The sales record counts what was actually closed on the phone. Both are accurate reports of different things.
5
The second goal reports the same wayOverall Sales - CRM reads $0 of $100. ActiveCampaign flags both goals “Needs attention.” The account has been telling you this since it was created.
What this means

Nothing here is a people problem. The measurement layer was never switched on — which means decisions have been made against a number that was never being collected.
