02 · Two Honest Flags

What the competitor map
forces us to confront.

Strategy that ignores the hard truths fails on contact. Two competitors define the boundaries of where TimeCells can win.

Flag 1 — The app twin Finelo

A near-identical app already exists: Finelo — 1M+ users, $20–40/mo, the same behavioral-finance learning loop. The product is not novel.

So: differentiation can't be app features. It has to be token / co-ownership + the DACH agent network — things Finelo structurally cannot copy.
Flag 2 — The reputational twin IM Academy

IM Academy — same shape (education + MLM + token, $175–325/mo) — is banned in Spain, Belgium & Italy, rated 1.5★, branded a pyramid. The path is a minefield.

So: a compliance + transparency spine isn't optional polish — it's the one thing that keeps TimeCells off IM Academy's road.

Two customers.

Revenue today flows almost entirely through agents. We arm and recruit them — we don't try to replace them.
